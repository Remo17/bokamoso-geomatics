01 — Introduction

Bokamoso Geomatics is a geomatics and land surveying company based in Rustenburg, North West. The practice is led by Kereng Senna, a registered Professional Land Surveyor, and serves municipal, public and private clients.

Our vision is to become a leading service provider of choice in geomatics — including cadastral and engineering surveys, mapping, land management and information, GIS and land reform — in Africa, and particularly the SADC region.

Our mission is to deliver excellent-quality service to our clients, grounded in ethics, honesty, integrity and teamwork.

About Bokamoso

02 — Services

Survey, map and manage land.

Five disciplines covering the full cycle of land information — from field measurement and cadastral definition to GIS and town planning.

01

Topographic Surveys

Survey and mapping of existing infrastructure, contours, and natural and man-made features.

02

Engineering Surveys

Setting out of roads and engineering structures, establishment of engineering control and benchmarks, and volumetric surveys including mine stockpiles.

03

Cadastral Surveys

Subdivision and consolidation, township establishment, servitudes and lease diagrams, sectional titles, beacon relocation and boundary confirmation, and property-area confirmation.

04

Geographic Information Systems

GIS data collection and capturing, GIS mapping, and data manipulation and analysis.

05

Land Management & Town Planning

Land audits, township establishments, subdivisions and consolidations, and land reform projects including tenure upgrades.

04 — Professional Expertise

Led by a registered Professional Land Surveyor.

Kereng Senna holds a National Diploma in Land Surveying from the Polytechnic of Namibia and a BSc in Land Surveying from the University of KwaZulu-Natal. His career includes experience at the Surveyor General’s Office, articles and training in geomatics, town and regional planning, and aerial surveying and photogrammetry.
