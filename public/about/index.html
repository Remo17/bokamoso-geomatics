About Bokamoso

A geomatics and land surveying practice based in Rustenburg, North West.

Bokamoso Geomatics provides cadastral, engineering and topographic surveys, GIS, land management and town planning services to municipal, public and private clients.

Fig. 01 — Aerial view, North West

01 — About Us

Precise land information for municipalities, developers and engineers.

Bokamoso Geomatics is a geomatics and land surveying company operating from Rustenburg, with a head office at Regus Business Park and a satellite office in Phatsima Township. The practice is directed by Kereng Senna, a registered Professional Land Surveyor.

Our work spans topographic, engineering and cadastral surveying, geographic information systems, and land management and town planning — including township establishment, subdivisions and consolidations, sectional titles and land reform projects.

02 — Vision

To become a leading service provider of choice in Geomatics — including Cadastral & Engineering Surveys, Mapping, Land Management & Information, GIS and Land Reform — in Africa, particularly the SADC region.

03 — Mission

To provide excellent-quality service to our clients, guided by ethics, honesty, integrity and teamwork.
